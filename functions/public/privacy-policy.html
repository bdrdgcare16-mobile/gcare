SERV Attendance Management Application

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

SERV Attendance Management Application ("SERV", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Employee Name
  • Employee ID
  • Company ID
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number (if provided)
  • Attendance logs and usage data
  • Location data during attendance operations
  • Device information (device type, OS version)
  • Biometric authentication status (not raw biometric data)

2. Location Information

We may collect location data during check-in, check-out, attendance verification, and tracking features (if enabled). This data is used strictly for attendance validation, employee tracking, and fraud prevention. Location data is not used for advertising or third-party tracking.

3. Biometric Authentication

The app may use fingerprint or face authentication for secure login. Biometric data is handled securely by the device operating system and is not stored on our servers. SERV only receives authentication success or failure status.

4. Notifications

The app may send notifications related to attendance reminders, task updates, system alerts, and background service activity. Users can manage notification settings in device preferences.

5. How We Use Data

We use collected data to manage attendance, verify identity, generate reports, improve application performance, and maintain system security.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data may be shared only with authorized organization administrators, within your organization, or when required by law.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including secure authentication, encrypted communication (HTTPS), and role-based access control. However, no method of internet transmission is completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain user data only as long as necessary for attendance management and organizational requirements. Data may be deleted upon request through your organization or support contact, subject to applicable policies.

9. User Rights

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their data through their organization administrator or by contacting support.

10. Third-Party Services

SERV uses Firebase services provided by Google, including authentication, database, and backend services. These services may collect limited technical data according to Google's policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

11. App Permissions

The app may request permissions such as location access, biometric authentication, and storage access (if required for features like uploads). These permissions are used strictly for application functionality.

12. Children’s Privacy

SERV is intended for organizational and employee use only. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under the age of 18.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date.
